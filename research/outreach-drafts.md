# Outreach drafts for Garhwali dataset access

These are drafts. Send them from the project owner's identity after inserting
the bracketed details.

## HimLingo structured export

Subject: Permissioned Garhwali dataset collaboration

Hello HimLingo team,

I am building a provenance-rich Garhwali language dataset for [research/project
description]. I would like to explore a permissioned, versioned export of the
Garhwali dictionary entries and, where contributors have authorized it,
example sentences and pronunciation audio.

Could we discuss a structured export containing terms, meanings, examples,
dialect/region, contributor or moderation provenance, and stable entry IDs? We
would also need an explicit dataset license and confirmation that contributor
terms permit redistribution and model-training use. CC BY 4.0 would work well
if it matches your contributors' permissions; we are open to a more limited
research agreement if it does not.

We will preserve attribution, version the source, report corrections, and keep
any unlicensed component outside the released corpus.

Regards,
[name and affiliation]
