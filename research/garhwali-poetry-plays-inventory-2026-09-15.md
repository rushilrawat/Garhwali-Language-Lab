# Garhwali poetry and plays inventory — 2026-09-15

## Current answer

Yes. Poetry and theatre are already represented, but mostly through university
study pages, bibliographies, historical references, and short extracts rather
than a complete open literary-text collection.

The later structured
[`garhwali-literary-works-catalog.json`](garhwali-literary-works-catalog.json)
adds all 61 named works from the user-supplied Itihaas Garhwali-language history,
including 14 modern poetry titles, 10 dramas, three poetry collections, eight
satire collections, prose, periodicals, early manuscripts, translations, and
digital language resources. It also preserves three oral-genre names and two
untitled source mentions in an explicit coverage audit.

biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeet_Singh_Negi), the [Chander Singh

release needs authoritative scans, page-level OCR, dialect/script tagging, and
permission or a compatible public-domain/open license for each edition.
